# How-to: Set Up Budgets

## Goal
Create category-based budgets to track your spending and avoid overspending each month.

---

## Create a Budget

1. Click **Budgets** in the sidebar
2. Click **Add Budget**
3. Fill in:
   - **Category:** Select "Food & Dining" (or any expense category)
   - **Amount:** `300` (your monthly limit)
   - **Period:** Monthly (resets each month)
4. Click **Save**

The budget appears in the list with a progress bar starting at 0%.

---

## Track Progress

As you record transactions in the "Food & Dining" category:

1. Go to **Budgets**
2. Watch the progress bar fill up
3. The **spent amount** and **remaining amount** update automatically

### Color Indicators

- **Green:** You're under budget (good!)
- **Yellow:** Getting close to the limit
- **Red:** Over budget — time to cut back

---

## Edit or Delete a Budget

- **Edit:** Click the budget → change amount or category → Save
- **Delete:** Click the trash icon → confirm

---

## Tips for Better Budgeting

### Use Specific Categories

Instead of one "Miscellaneous" budget, create specific ones:
- Food & Dining: $300
- Transportation: $150
- Entertainment: $100
- Housing: $1200

### Set Realistic Amounts

Look at your **Reports → Spending** to see what you actually spent last month. Set budgets slightly below that to save more.

### Check the Dashboard

Your **Dashboard** shows a budget summary widget — a quick way to see if you're on track without opening the Budgets page.

---

## Confirmation

- Budget appears in the list with correct amount
- Progress bar updates as you add transactions in that category
- Over-budget indicators appear when you exceed the limit

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | Solution |
|----------|----------|
| **Budget not showing progress** | Ensure transactions are categorized correctly. The category name must match exactly. |
| **Can't create budget without category** | Currently, every budget must be linked to a category. Create a "General" category if you want a catch-all budget. |
| **Progress bar seems wrong** | Check the date range — budgets reset monthly. Transactions from last month don't count toward this month's budget. |
