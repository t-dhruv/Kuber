# Setting up a Budget

A Budget in Kuber is an amount assigned to a Category for one month. Spending is
matched against it from your Transactions, so a Budget is only as good as your
categorisation — see [Categories](categories.md) first if Transactions are still
landing uncategorised.

Go to **Budget** in the sidebar, under Planning. The arrows either side of the
month name move between months; each month holds its own amounts.

## Budget a Category

Click **Add Budget** and fill in:

- **Category** — one Category per Budget.
- **Budget amount** — for this month.
- **Budget type** — see below.

### Budget types

The type changes how Kuber reads the Budget, and the three are summarised on the
page as Fixed, Flexible and Non-Monthly:

- **Fixed** — recurring, same amount every month: rent, phone, insurance. A
  variance here means something actually changed.
- **Flexible** — variable month to month: groceries, dining. Some variance is
  normal.
- **Non-Monthly** — periodic: annual or quarterly costs. Spending arrives in one
  month rather than spread evenly, so a zero-spend month is not underspending.

Pick honestly. The summary rows total Fixed, Flexible and Non-Monthly separately,
and that split is the point — it is what tells you how much of your outgoings you
could actually change.

## Adjust amounts quickly

Budget amounts are editable in place. Click the amount on a Category row, type a
new one, and it saves — you do not need to reopen the modal for each change.

To budget a Category inside an existing group, use **Add category to** that
group, which pre-selects the group for you.

## Read the page

**Left to budget** across the top is income minus everything you have assigned.
Green with "left to budget" means income is not yet fully assigned; red with
"over budget" means you have assigned more than you expect to earn. The banner
also carries your actual income and savings rate.

Kuber flags Categories with spending but no Budget in an alert above the table,
with a control to budget each one without leaving the page. That alert is the
fastest route to a Budget that reflects your real spending.

Each row shows the amount budgeted against the amount actually spent.

## AI Budget Coach

If an AI provider is configured, **Analyse my budget** produces tips for the
current month, streamed into the page. It reads the month's Budgets and spending
and suggests where the numbers look unrealistic. Kuber works fully without it —
see [the AI advisor](ai-advisor.md) for what configuring a provider involves and
what data it sends.

## Verify

- The Category shows your amount budgeted, and its spend for the month beside it.
- **Left to budget** has fallen by the amount you assigned.
- The Category no longer appears in the unbudgeted alert.
- Moving to next month shows the Budget you set for that month, not this one's.
