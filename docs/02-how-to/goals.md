# Savings Goals and debt payoff

**Goals** in the sidebar, under Planning, tracks two opposite things: money you
are accumulating toward a target, and debt you are paying down toward zero.

## Create a savings Goal

Click **New Goal** and fill in:

- **Type** — Savings, Vacation, Home Down Payment, Wedding, Education, Car or
  Other. This sets the icon and colour; it does not change any arithmetic.
- **Goal name** — "Emergency Fund", "Bali Trip".
- **Target amount** — what you are aiming for.
- **Target date** — optional.
- **Starting amount** — what you have already put aside. Leave at 0 if starting
  from nothing.
- **Monthly contribution** — optional, what you intend to add each month.

The Goal appears as a card with a progress bar of current against target.

## Record progress

Goals do not fill themselves. Recording a Transaction into a savings Account does
not move a Goal — the two are tracked separately, on purpose, so a Goal can span
Accounts or track something with no Account at all.

Click **Add funds** on the Goal card and enter an amount. The progress bar and
the remaining figure update.

## Track a debt payoff

**New Debt Goal** is the mirror image: the target is the balance you owe, and
progress is what you have repaid. The card shows the total, the amount repaid,
and what remains.

Give it a **monthly payment** and the debt panel projects a debt-free date from
your total debt and total monthly payments across every debt Goal. It is a
straight-line projection — it assumes you keep paying that amount, and it does
not model interest.

Record repayments with **Make Payment** on the debt Goal card — the same flow as
**Add funds**, named for what it is.

## Edit or delete a Goal

The **⋯** menu on a Goal card holds edit and delete. Deleting a Goal removes the
target and its recorded contributions; it does not touch any Transaction or
Account.

## Verify

- The Goal card shows the right target, and the progress bar reflects your
  starting amount.
- A contribution moves both the progress bar and the remaining figure.
- A debt Goal with a monthly payment shows a debt-free projection.
